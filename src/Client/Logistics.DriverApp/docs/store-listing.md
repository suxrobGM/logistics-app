## Description (4000 chars max - iOS & Android)

```text
LogisticsX Driver is the professional companion app for truck drivers and owner-operators using the LogisticsX fleet management platform. Built for speed and reliability on the road, it gives you instant access to everything you need - without the paperwork.

KNOW YOUR LOADS BEFORE YOU ROLL
View all assigned loads and trips at a glance. See pickup and delivery details, stop-by-stop instructions, customer information, and status updates - all in one place.

NAVIGATE WITH ONE TAP
Open turn-by-turn directions to any pickup, delivery, or stop directly in Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android). No copy-pasting addresses. No switching apps.

REAL-TIME STATUS UPDATES
Mark loads as picked up, in transit, or delivered as you go. Dispatch and customers are kept informed automatically - no phone calls needed.

STAY IN SYNC
Get instant notifications the moment an assignment changes. Message dispatch directly in the app. Never miss an update, even when you're on the road.

BUILT FOR PROFESSIONAL DRIVERS
- Clean, distraction-free interface designed for use on the go
- Works with your existing LogisticsX account - no separate signup
- Secure login and role-based access
- Background location tracking for real-time delivery visibility
- Supports intermodal container and vehicle transport operations

HOW IT WORKS
Loads are assigned in the LogisticsX TMS portal - by your dispatcher if you drive for a fleet, or by you if you run your own authority. They arrive in the app instantly, complete with route details and instructions. Update statuses as you deliver, and the portal reflects it in real time.

LogisticsX Driver requires an account on the LogisticsX platform. If you drive for a company that uses LogisticsX, they can add you. If you run your own authority, you can get your own account at logisticsx.app - the platform has a solo mode built for one-truck carriers.
```

## Notes

- Screenshots should show: Login screen, Loads list, Load detail, Trip detail with stops, Map navigation
- The app requires a LogisticsX account - state it clearly to avoid bad reviews from random installs. Don't phrase it as "ask your dispatcher": owner-operators are their own dispatcher, and telling them to ask someone else reads as "this app isn't for you"
- Consider adding "For LogisticsX platform users only" to the first line of description if getting irrelevant installs
- Keyword list leans fleet/dispatch. If owner-operator installs matter, swap one term for `owner operator`
